FLOCK TOGETHER — GRUB CARD DATA (24 total)
=================================================================
Instructions: Grubs are a single 24-card pool, evenly split 12/12 into two
shared location decks (Inside/Outside) at deal time and again whenever both
decks empty out and get reshuffled — no individual card is permanently
"Inside" or "Outside," so there's no Location field to transcribe here; the
12/12 split is just a dealing rule the engine applies at runtime. Grubs
live in these shared decks rather than a player's hand — no hand limit, and
one gets discarded from either deck (player's choice) at the end of every
day.

Every Grub has Health, and (per confirmation) killing one grants the
attacker who lands the final blow a Reward/benefit — same shape as a
Predator's Loot Drop, just card-specific instead of species-specific. Most
Grubs also have a dice-roll Effect that triggers on attack (a defend/
retaliation roll, e.g. "1-2: Lose 1 health") — some of these are minor
damage-back, some may say "nothing," and some cards may have no roll effect
at all.

One line per card, in this format:

  N. Name - Health: <N> - Effect: <roll text, or "none"> - Reward: <text>

Health: the heart/damage-capacity value printed on the card.

Effect: the dice-roll defend/retaliation text triggered when the Grub is
attacked, copied exactly as printed (e.g. "1-2: Lose 1 health"). If a card
has no such effect, write "none" — don't guess or invent one.

Reward: the benefit granted to whoever lands the killing blow, copied
exactly as printed.

Leave a field as [?] if you can't find/read it and want to skip for now —
partial progress is fine, this file gets fully re-read each time you say
it's updated.
